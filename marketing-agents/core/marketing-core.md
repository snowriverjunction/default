# Marketing Core

Shared editorial standards and QA gate for all companies under this worker.

---

## Editorial law

- No em dashes (---, --). Use a colon, a period, or restructure the sentence.
- No fabricated statistics. Every number must trace to a named, published source.
- No passive-voice hedging that obscures the point.
- Plain language. If a sentence requires re-reading, shorten it.

---

## Core QA gate

Run these checks on every deliverable before writing to review/. Fix every fail.

| # | Check | Pass condition |
|---|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Em dash | Zero instances of — or -- |
| 2 | Statistics sourced | Every figure has an inline source label |
| 3 | Tier 3 figures | None present unless tied to a named published source |
| 4 | Fabrication | No invented numbers or unnamed "studies" |
| 5 | CTA present | At least one clear call to action |
| 6 | Word count | Within the range specified by the playbook |
| 7 | Brand voice | Matches the tone defined in brand.md |

## AI tell list (phrases to eliminate)

- "in today's landscape"
- "in the ever-evolving"
- "it's important to note"
- "leverage" (as a verb, unless quoting a source)
- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "seamless"
- "cutting-edge"
- "robust" (unless describing a technical spec)
- "delve into"
- "I cannot and will not"
- Rhetorical questions used as transitions

---

## Source tier definitions

**Tier 1** -- Primary government or regulatory data: Statistics Canada, Bank of Canada, OSFI, CMHC official reports. Citable without qualification.

**Tier 2** -- Industry bodies and recognized aggregators: Equifax Canada, CREA, CMP (Canadian Mortgage Professional), CMT (Canadian Mortgage Trends), Mortgage Professionals Canada (MPC). Citable with source label.

**Tier 3** -- Internal estimates, vendor-produced research, or unverified survey figures. NOT usable until the worker locates the original published source and records it. Figures currently in Tier 3 holding: "70 percent of Canadians renewing in 2025", "60 percent of mortgage holders unaware of white-label products", "82 percent broker-client retention rate". Flag and cut if not sourced.
