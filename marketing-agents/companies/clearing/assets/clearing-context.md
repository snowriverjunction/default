## What Clearing is

A financial operating system for short-term rental property managers. It sits between the property management software and the bank, and between the manager and their homeowners. It is not a property management software, and a PMS is not required to use it. Clearing serves the US market.

## Product modules

- Automated bookkeeping and reconciliation: pull transactions from Stripe, Airbnb, bank accounts, and credit cards into one place, enriched and reconciled, to close the books faster.
- Trust accounting: per-homeowner sub-balances, collect funds, and pay homeowners, set up correctly for multi-owner operations.
- Expense management: track and categorize property and supply expenses.
- Reporting: owner and accountant portals with clean statements.

## Banking and compliance structure

Clearing is a financial technology company, not a bank. Deposit accounts are issued by Evolve Bank & Trust, Member FDIC. A business can hold a main balance insured up to $250,000, attached to one or more zero-balance accounts that each have unique account numbers for receiving funds. Funds flow into the main balance.

Disclosure to include wherever funds, deposits, balances, or insurance are mentioned: Clearing is a financial technology company and not a bank; deposit accounts are issued by Evolve Bank & Trust, Member FDIC. ## Pricing (confirm current)

Entry around $25 per month for up to 5 listings, scaling by unit count. Trust accounting as an add-on around $2.50 per unit per month, with a one-time implementation fee near $199. Annual billing discounted about 20%. Two tiers historically: a bookkeeping core (Pro) and the full trust-accounting stack (Ultimate). Verify all figures before quoting them.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning | Copy guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Trust accounting | Holding and tracking money on behalf of others (homeowners) separately from the operator's own funds. | Clearing's primary product; the compliance backbone. |
| Sub-balance | A per-homeowner balance within a managed account. | Core trust-accounting capability. |
| Commingling | Mixing client or owner funds with operating funds, a key compliance risk. | The risk trust accounting prevents; do not give legal advice. |
| Reconciliation | Matching recorded transactions to bank and processor records. | Automated by Clearing. |
| Owner statement | The financial report a manager sends each homeowner. | Transparency and retention driver. |
| ACH | Electronic bank-to-bank transfer in the US. | How homeowners are paid. |
| FDIC | US deposit insurance via member banks. | Only in the banking disclosure, tied to Evolve Bank & Trust. |
| PMS | Property management software. | Clearing integrates with these; it is not one. |
| STR | Short-term rental. | The core market. |
