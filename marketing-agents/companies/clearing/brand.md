## Positioning

Clearing is purpose-built trust accounting and automated bookkeeping for short-term rental
(STR) property managers. It brings together cashflow from Airbnb, Stripe, VRBO, and other
platforms, automates reconciliation, generates owner statements, and supports per-owner
sub-balances to prevent commingling of funds.

The core promise: close the books faster, stay compliant with trust-accounting obligations,
and give homeowners transparent, accurate statements -- without the manual work.

---

## Target audience

- Short-term rental property managers (from boutique operators to scaled portfolios)
- STR companies that manage properties on behalf of homeowners and must handle funds in trust
- Finance-minded operators who have outgrown spreadsheets or generic bookkeeping tools

---

## Voice and tone

- **Calm and precise.** The audience handles other people's money. Anxiety and hype are both
  wrong. Steady expertise is right.
- **Benefit-led.** Name the chore or risk first, then show how Clearing addresses it.
- **Relief, not excitement.** The reader wants fewer problems, not a bigger dream.
- **Plain language.** Avoid jargon where plain words work. When regulation terms are needed,
  define them briefly.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, colons, or new sentences.
- **No fabricated statistics.**

---

## Required banking disclosure

Whenever funds, deposits, balances, or FDIC insurance are mentioned, include this disclosure
verbatim (or a natural-language equivalent that contains every required element):

> Clearing is a financial technology company and not a bank. Deposit accounts are issued by
> Evolve Bank & Trust, Member FDIC. Balances are insured up to $250,000.

Never call Clearing a bank. Never overstate coverage (do not say "fully insured" without
specifying the $250,000 limit). Never generalize FDIC coverage to amounts above $250,000.

## Key product capabilities (sourced from getclearing.co, June 2026)

- **Trust accounting**: purpose-built ledger for STR managers; separates owner funds from
  management fees; supports state trust-accounting compliance workflows
- **Sub-balances / sub-ledgers**: per-owner or per-property-group segregated balances within
  the main account; eliminates commingling of funds; transparent fund management
- **Automated bookkeeping**: auto-imports and categorizes transactions from connected
  platforms and bank accounts
- **Multi-source cashflow tracking**: Airbnb, Stripe, VRBO, bank accounts, credit cards, and
  more consolidated in one view
- **Owner statements**: generate statements at scale across all owners; pay homeowners
  directly from the owner statement
- **Reconciliation**: booking revenue reconciled against bank deposits; Chrome extension
  available to assist reconciliation workflows
- **Trust accounting field**: transaction-level control to assign responsibility (owner,
  management company, or excluded)
- **Vendor payments**: pay homeowners and vendors from within the platform
