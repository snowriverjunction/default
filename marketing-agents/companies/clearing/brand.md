## Brand essence

"Clearing stands for clarity in every dollar."

Purpose-built for trust accounting, compliance, and the operators who take both seriously.

**What Clearing is**: the financial operating layer for STR. An AI-powered trust accounting
and bookkeeping automation platform built specifically for short-term rental property
managers. Reservation-driven chaos turned into accurate books, compliant distributions, and
audit-ready reporting.

**What Clearing is not**: a general-purpose bookkeeping tool retrofitted for STR. Not a
property management system. Not a generic accounting platform.

**Why it matters**: OTAs bundle reservation revenue and net fees. Payout timing varies.
Owners demand clean statements. Trust accounting rules require funds tracked by property
and stakeholder. No PMS was built to solve this. Clearing was.

## Writing rules

Always:
- Lead with outcomes. "Close your books in hours" before "automated reconciliation engine."
- Use STR-specific workflow language: trust accounting, payout reconciliation, bookkeeping,
  owner distributions, ACH, audit trail, three-way reconciliation.
- Include enterprise credibility signals naturally: audit-ready, role-based access, financial
  controls, reconciliation integrity, general ledger.
- Be specific. "$40+ hours/month down to under 20" is more powerful than "saves significant
  time."
- Write in active voice. "Clearing reconciles your payouts" not "payouts are reconciled."
- Distinguish between bookkeeping and reconciliation -- they are related but not the same.

Never:
- Use em dashes anywhere in copy. Use commas, periods, or colons instead.
- Use manufacturing fear as the primary driver. Urgency grounded in real pain is fine.
  Panic-based language is not Clearing's voice.
- Directly attack PMS platforms by name or implication in external content.
- Overstate the AI. Describe what it produces (clean, reconciled, audit-ready books), not
  what it is.
- Use generic accounting software language: "end-to-end platform," "comprehensive financial
  management solution."
- Write paragraphs that could apply to any fintech. Every sentence should only make sense
  coming from Clearing.

---

## On urgency and fear

Fear is a real motivator in accounting and compliance contexts. Content that speaks honestly
to the risk of inaccurate books, audit exposure, or losing financial history in a PMS
migration is appropriate when grounded in genuine stakes. The line is between real
operational pain (which Clearing can speak to directly) and manufactured panic (which it
avoids). A prospect who realizes their current process cannot survive an audit is ready to
act -- meet that moment honestly.

---

## On AI

Describe what Clearing's AI produces: clean, reconciled, audit-ready books. Do not hype the
technology itself. "AI-powered trust accounting" is the category label -- use it where
needed. Do not say "revolutionary AI" or describe the model. Let the output speak.

---

## PMS and portability framing

Do say:
- Clearing integrates with leading PMS platforms. The operations layer and financial layer
  work together -- and independently when needed.
- Your accounting history travels with you. If you change your PMS, Clearing keeps your
  full audit trail intact.
- Operators in Clearing can evaluate and switch PMS platforms with far less financial
  disruption. That's a feature, not an afterthought.

Never say:
- "PMS platforms can't do accounting." (Adversarial framing.)
- "Your PMS is the problem."
- Anything that directly attacks a named PMS in external content.
- Anything that makes an operator feel bad about their current setup.

---

## Required banking disclosure

Whenever funds, deposits, balances, or FDIC insurance are mentioned, include this disclosure
verbatim (or a natural-language equivalent that contains every required element):

> Clearing is a financial technology company and not a bank. Deposit accounts are issued by
> Evolve Bank & Trust, Member FDIC. Balances are insured up to $250,000.

Never call Clearing a bank. Never overstate coverage. Never generalize FDIC coverage to
amounts above $250,000.

## Audience map

**Primary: STR Operators and Property Managers**
Managing 10-200+ properties; finance workflow has become a constraint on growth. Spending
20-40+ hours/month on manual reconciliation, statements, and payout matching. Using PMS
platforms; wants a financial layer that integrates cleanly and works independently.
Messaging: lead with speed of close, accuracy, peace of mind, and scalability.

**Power user: Finance Leads, Accountants, and Bookkeepers**
Responsible for compliance, trust accounting accuracy, and year-end close. Need role-based
access, audit trails, clean exports, and reconciliation integrity. Evaluates platforms on
financial controls, general ledger accuracy, and audit-readiness.
Messaging: lead with trust accounting rigor, three-way reconciliation, P&L accuracy, and
audit-ready reporting.

**End beneficiary: Homeowners and Investors**
Invited to their own read-only portal to review statements, reservations, and balances.
Want transparency without complexity. Their experience of Clearing reflects directly on the
property manager's brand and credibility.
Messaging: lead with visibility, clarity, and confidence their investment is handled
correctly.

**Ecosystem: Vendors and Service Providers**
Invited to submit invoices via vendor portal. Care about getting paid accurately and on
time.
Messaging: lead with simplicity, payment speed, and transparent status.

## Visual style

Do use:
- Actual Clearing product screenshots: payout reconciliation, owner statements, transaction
  feeds, audit trails.
- Money-flow diagrams showing how funds move across OTAs, Clearing, owners, vendors, and
  bank accounts.
- Dark navy gradient backgrounds with teal accents for hero and brand contexts.
- Abstract data visualization: bar charts, flow nodes, clean dashboards suggesting financial
  order.
- Rounded surfaces (12-16px border radius), subtle shadows, polished fintech feel.

Never use:
- Generic stock photography of accountants, spreadsheets, or office workers.
- Lifestyle photography of vacation rental properties (that is PMS visual language, not
  Clearing's).
- Decorative illustrations that do not connect to the product or financial theme.
- Busy, cluttered layouts. Clearing's design principle is precision and spaciousness.
- More than 3 brand colors in a single composition.
