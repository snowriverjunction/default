## Product overview

Clearing is a complete trust accounting and automated bookkeeping platform for short-term
rental (STR) property managers. It centralizes cashflow from multiple booking and payment
platforms, automates reconciliation, generates owner statements, and provides per-owner
sub-balances to prevent commingling of funds.

## Confirmed product features (as of June 2026)

**Trust accounting**
- Purpose-built ledger separating owner funds from management fees
- Trust accounting field: per-transaction assignment (owner / management company / excluded)
- Supports state trust-accounting compliance workflows
- Prevents commingling of funds

**Sub-balances**
- Per-owner or per-property-group segregated balances within the main account
- Each sub-ledger represents a distinct balance for a specific homeowner or property group
- Transparent fund management; eliminates the single-pool commingling risk

**Automated bookkeeping**
- Auto-imports and categorizes transactions from connected platforms and bank accounts
- Reconciles booking revenue against bank deposits

**Owner statements**
- Generate statements at scale for all owners
- Pay homeowners directly from the owner statement

**Multi-source cashflow**
- Airbnb, Stripe, VRBO, bank accounts, credit cards consolidated in one dashboard

**Vendor payments**
- Pay homeowners and vendors from within the platform

**Chrome extension**
- Assists reconciliation workflows (VRBO and other platforms)
