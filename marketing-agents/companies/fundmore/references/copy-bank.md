> What's the hardest part of your current audit trail to defend?

## Power Phrases (reusable across personas)

**Compliance as infrastructure:**
OSFI's B-20 requirements aren't a compliance checkbox. They're a structural constraint
that every mortgage decision in Canada has to pass through. FundMore builds that constraint
into the decisioning layer, not the audit layer. Compliance is the architecture, not the
afterthought.

**Decision speed as competitive moat:**
The lender who closes in two days while competitors take ten wins three things: the deal,
the relationship, and the referral. Speed in lending isn't an operational metric. It's a
revenue metric.

**Consistency as risk reduction:**
Manual underwriting introduces variance. Variance introduces regulatory exposure.
Every decision Fathom makes follows the same logic, documented the same way. That's not
just efficiency. That's defensibility.
