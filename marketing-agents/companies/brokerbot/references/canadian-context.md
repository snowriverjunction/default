# Canadian Mortgage Market Context

Reference material for grounding BrokerBot content in the Canadian market. Worker reads this every run.

---

## Key regulatory context

**OSFI Guideline B-20** -- The federal stress test. Requires borrowers to qualify at the higher of the contract rate plus 2% or 5.25%. Affects renewal strategy for clients with existing mortgages.

**Renewal cliff** -- Refers to the large volume of Canadian mortgages originally written at 2020-2021 pandemic-era rates (sub-2%) coming up for renewal at materially higher rates. CMHC and Bank of Canada have both published on the scale of this cohort.

**Big Six** -- Royal Bank of Canada (RBC), Toronto-Dominion (TD), Bank of Nova Scotia (Scotiabank), Bank of Montreal (BMO), Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce (CIBC), National Bank of Canada. These are the dominant direct-to-consumer mortgage originators that brokers compete against at renewal.

**Mortgage Stress Test** -- Applied by federally regulated lenders under B-20. Applies to both insured and uninsured mortgages since 2018.

## Approved Tier 2 sources (cite by name and date)

| Source | What to cite it for |
|--------|---------------------|
| CMHC (Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation) | Housing starts, arrears rates, renewal cohort data |
| Bank of Canada | Overnight rate decisions, Monetary Policy Reports, household debt data |
| CREA (Canadian Real Estate Association) | National and regional home sales, average prices |
| Equifax Canada | Credit metrics, mortgage delinquency, consumer debt |
| Statistics Canada | Labour force, income, demographic data |
| OSFI | Regulatory guidance, B-20 updates |
| CMP (Canadian Mortgage Professional magazine) | Industry survey data, broker volume benchmarks |
| CMT (Canadian Mortgage Trends) | Rate tracking, lender news, market commentary |
| Mortgage Professionals Canada (MPC) | Annual state-of-the-industry reports, broker count data |

Always record: exact figure, source name, report title or URL, and release date.

## What NOT to do with Canadian context

- Do not cite US Federal Reserve data as a proxy for Bank of Canada policy.
- Do not use "North America" when the point is specifically Canadian.
- Do not cite CMHC housing starts for a point about mortgage volume -- they measure different things.
- Do not use a figure from a prior year's MPC report without checking if a newer edition is available.
