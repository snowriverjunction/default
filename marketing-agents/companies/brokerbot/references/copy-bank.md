# BrokerBot Copy Bank

Approved phrases, hooks, and sentence patterns the worker may reuse and remix.

---

## Approved hooks (loss aversion)

- "Your client renews in 90 days. The bank already emailed them."
- "Every broker knows that face -- the client who didn't call before signing with the bank."
- "The renewal cliff isn't coming. It's here."
- "White-label means your name on the screen, not BrokerBot's."
- "You closed the deal. Someone else is working the renewal."

---

## Approved hooks (revenue math)

- "One retained renewal at $400K is $2,400 in your pocket. How many are you leaving?"
- "Average broker loses 3-5 renewals a year to bank direct. At a 1% referral average, that's $15,000 gone."
  SOURCE REQUIRED before use -- this is a directional estimate, not a published figure. Flag until sourced.

---

## Approved proof framing

- "According to [Source, date], [stat]. That's [plain-English implication for brokers]."
- "CMHC projects [X]. Brokers who [action] before [trigger] capture the referral."

---

## Approved CTAs

- "Book a Demo -- 20 minutes, no pitch deck."
- "Reply with DEMO and I'll send a calendar link."
- "Link in bio to see it running live."
- "DM me -- I'll show you the client-facing side in 10 minutes."

## Angles to avoid (seed list)

- Feature-first leads (what BrokerBot does before what the broker loses)
- US market comparisons without explicit Canadian bridging
