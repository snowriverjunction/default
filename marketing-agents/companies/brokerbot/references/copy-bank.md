## Power Phrases (reusable across verticals)

**Loss Aversion Hook:** 70% of past clients don't return. Not because they hated your service. Because they forgot you existed. Meanwhile, 60% of mortgage deals come from referrals and repeat clients. Without consistent follow-up, your book of business becomes someone else's payday.

**Revenue Math Hook:** The revenue math doesn't lie. At $5,784 per loan, losing 400 deals equals $2.3M evaporated. BrokerBot clients typically see a 29%+ retention boost in Year 1. Break-even happens at just four retained clients.

**Behavioral Insight Hook:** People check property values like stocks. 82% of homeowners want monthly home value updates. Be where your clients already are: thinking about real estate.
> NOTE: The source brand kit also cites "referral trust: 82%." That is the same figure used for two different metrics. Verify both numbers against the source data before publishing; do not assume they are identical.

**Canadian Differentiation Hook:** A US platform with a maple leaf sticker is not a Canadian platform. BrokerBot is built on 18M+ Canadian property records from OPTA/FCT, designed for 5-year renewal cycles, PIPEDA-compliant, bilingual, and integrated with the tools Canadian brokers actually use.
