Colour Application Rules

Light-First, Dark-Accent Strategy

Default canvas: white. All main site pages, product UI, marketing pages, and collateral default to white (#FFFFFF) or soft gray (#F8F9FB) backgrounds.

Dark sections for impact. Use #0B1818 for hero banners, stat callout strips, CTA sections, and pricing cards where pink-on-dark contrast creates maximum punch. Think of these as "spotlight" moments, not the entire stage.

Pink (#FC0A7E) is for action. CTAs, key stats, interactive elements, hover states. Never as a large background fill.

Purple (#86308B) is being tested for buttons. A/B test purple vs. pink for primary CTA buttons starting with the pricing page. Purple differentiates BrokerBot from the "every SaaS uses pink" crowd and ties into the logo's secondary colour.

Logo treatment: Logo_light (purple icon + dark text) on white/light backgrounds. Logo_dark (white icon + white text) on dark or pink backgrounds.

Button Placement Guidelines

When to Use Each Style

Filled buttons for primary actions: "Book a Demo," "Start Free Trial," "Get Started," pricing tier CTAs.

Outline buttons for secondary actions: "Learn More," "See Features," "Watch Demo," when paired next to a filled CTA.

On dark sections: Use white-filled buttons with pink or purple text, or pink-filled buttons with white text. Never use outline buttons on dark backgrounds.

Icon buttons: Icon right-justified, text left-justified, 20px padding. Same height/radius specs.

Voice Pillars

How we always sound

Numbers-first. Lead with data. "$2.3M evaporated" hits harder than "you could lose revenue." Always quantify the pain.

Direct and declarative. Short sentences. Active voice. "BrokerBot fixes that. Automatically." No hedging.

Practitioner-level. We speak to dealmakers, not C-suites. Use industry terms: "book of business," "renewal," "refi," "HELOC."

Canadian identity. We reference PIPEDA, B-20, Filogix, 5-year terms, and CASL. We are not a US product with a maple leaf sticker.

Tone Modulation

How tone shifts by context

Website / Ads: Maximum punch. Loss aversion. "70% of past clients don't return."

Email / Nurture: Warm but urgent. "Your clients are checking home values right now."

Sales / Demo: Confident, proof-heavy. "29% retention vs. 20% average."

Support / Onboarding: Patient, clear, encouraging. "Upload your list. BrokerBot does the rest."

Enterprise / Lender: Slightly more formal, ROI-centric. "Reduce borrower attrition with automated equity intelligence."

10 / Brand Do's & Don'ts
Protect the Brand
These rules ensure BrokerBot looks, sounds, and feels consistent across every touchpoint.

Brand Do's

  • Default to white/light backgrounds for all pages and materials
  • Use dark (#0B1818) sections strategically for hero banners, stat strips, and CTA blocks
  • Use #FC0A7E (pink) for CTAs and highlights; test #86308B (purple) buttons on pricing
  • Lead with pain/loss aversion in headlines, then pivot to the solution
  • Quantify everything: $5,784, 29%, 18M+, 82%
  • Speak to practitioners: "book of business," "renewal," "refi"
  • Reference Canadian context: PIPEDA, B-20, 5-year terms, Filogix
  • Use Gothic A1 for headings and Mulish for body (Google Fonts only)
  • Include "re engage" tagline with the logo where space permits
  • Button specs: 50px height, 3px radius, 20px min padding, always

Brand Don'ts

  • Never default to all-dark page designs (dark is for accent sections only)
  • Never use buzzwords: "leverage," "synergize," "cutting-edge," "robust," "best-in-class"
  • Never use em dashes. Use commas, colons, semicolons, or rewrite instead
  • Never put BrokerBot's brand ahead of the customer's brand
  • Never use stock photos of handshakes or generic office scenes
  • Never compare directly to HomeBot by name publicly (say "US platforms")
  • Never promise specific ROI without citing a published case study
  • Never use passive voice in headlines
  • Never substitute non-Google fonts for Gothic A1 or Mulish
  • Never modify the logo colours outside purple (#86308B) and white

Logo Usage

  • Logo_light (purple icon + dark text): white/light backgrounds
  • Logo_dark (white icon + white text): dark or coloured backgrounds
  • Logo_small: nav bars, tight spaces, app icons
  • Export: PNG @2x and SVG. Favicon: ICO at 64x64px
  • Minimum 120px wide digital, 1 inch print. Maintain aspect ratio.

Logo Misuse

  • Never place logo on busy backgrounds without a solid overlay
  • Never rotate, skew, add drop shadows, or effects
  • Never change icon colour from purple or white
  • Never use low-res. Always SVG or PNG @2x
  • Never separate the icon from the wordmark in formal usage
