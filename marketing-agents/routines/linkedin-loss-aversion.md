# Routine: LinkedIn Loss-Aversion Post

Schedule: weekly, Wednesday 08:00 ET

---

Run the BrokerBot playbook "LinkedIn loss-aversion post".

1. Pick this week's ICP (rotate the five; mortgage brokers most often) and one hook (loss
   aversion, revenue math, behavioural insight, or Canadian differentiation). Check
   memory/brokerbot-memory.md for angles approved or killed recently and avoid repeats.
2. Draft on the LinkedIn scaffold: hook before the fold, the uncomfortable truth, the
   white-label fix in one sentence, one proof stat, a direct CTA. Any stat must trace to
   the approved source library. Do not use a Tier 3 figure unless it is sourced.
3. Run the core QA gate, then the BrokerBot Pre-Flight.
4. Write review/linkedin-<date>.md, Status NEEDS REVIEW. Log the run.

Do not publish.
