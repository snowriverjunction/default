# Routine: Dreaming Pass (the Learning Loop)

Schedule: weekly, Friday 16:00 ET (after the week's content runs)

Run the Clearing dreaming pass.

1. Read the past 7 days of companies/clearing/review/ packages, any human edits or lines
   beginning with NOTES:, and memory/run-log.md.
2. Extract patterns: recurring voice corrections, any missed banking disclosure or
   overstated claim, angles approved vs killed, stats flagged, formats that performed.
3. Write a dated PROPOSED update to memory/memory-proposed.md. Operational preferences only:
   topic-rotation notes, sourcing and disclosure reminders, do and don't refinements. This
   is memory, not brand law.
4. Do NOT edit core/marketing-core.md, companies/clearing/brand.md, or the editorial law.
   List any canon change under "For human decision" instead of applying it.
5. Self-grade the week's drafts against the core QA gate and the Clearing Pre-Flight, with
   special attention to the banking disclosure, and note any recurring failure.

A human reviews memory-proposed.md and merges approved items into memory.md. Nothing takes
effect until merged.

Do not publish.
